The Founder's True Story

"It Started When I Saw My Nearby Forest Destroyed—While Anger Was Burning Me Inside."

The Garden wasn't dreamed up in a corporate boardroom. It was born from two emergencies happening at the exact same time:

  • 1. Saving My Local Forest & River:
    I watched the forest near my home get cut down and destroyed. I wanted to raise continuous funding for anti-deforestation agencies and river protection, so I decided to build an app dedicated to funding nature.
  • 2. Solving My Own Anger Management:
    At the same time, I struggled with my own anger. I realized Emotional Intelligence could help me, but the real problem was having no partner who could truly understand my emotions in the moment—and no simple way to gain awareness before anger took over.

"Just like a forest gets destroyed when we neglect it, our relationships and peace of mind get destroyed when we don't understand our own emotions."

When you are angry or overwhelmed, nobody wants to type into a blank notes app. You need to talk—and you need a partner that listens without judgment, tells you what emotion is actually driving your anger, and gives you a practical way to cool down.

That is why I merged both missions into The Garden. Every time you record a voice note, our AI builds your Daily Emotion Chart and teaches you how to manage your emotions. And 30% of every dollar we make goes straight to deforestation agencies and river protection—so healing your inner world literally heals the Earth.

How Emotional Intelligence Works

From Raw Voice Note to Emotional Mastery

You cannot manage an emotion you don't understand. Here is how The Garden acts as your daily emotional intelligence partner:

01

Speak Your Voice Note

Press record and talk freely about what made you angry, anxious, or exhausted. Speaking out loud immediately reduces pressure in the brain's emotional center.

02

AI Emotion Charting

Our AI analyzes both your words and vocal tone (pitch, sighs, speed) to chart which emotions are highest today—revealing hidden burnout or hurt behind your anger.

03

Instant Calming Solutions

Your AI partner gives you an immediate, personalized solution for that exact emotion—such as a 90-second anger reset, a boundary script, or a breathing guide.

04

Paid Psychiatrist Calls

When your Emotion Chart shows heavy or long-term patterns, tap once to book a paid 1-on-1 video call with a licensed psychiatrist for professional care.
